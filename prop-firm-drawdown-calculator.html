The four ways prop firms measure drawdown

Every firm's rules page says "maximum drawdown" or "max loss", but the wording hides four different mechanics. Knowing which one you are on changes how much room you actually have before you size the next trade.

TypeWhere the floor isFirms (check your plan)

The intraday version is the one that surprises people: a trade that went +$1,500 unrealised and closed at +$300 still moved your floor up by $1,500. On a static plan that trade cost you nothing in room.

Daily loss: it is measured from the day's starting balance, and it can include floating losses

At FTMO and FundedNext the daily limit is a percentage of the balance at the daily reset (00:00 CE(S)T at FTMO, server midnight at FundedNext), and the check includes unrealised P&L. That is why the calculator asks for your balance at the start of today and your open P&L: the daily-loss room shown is after counting what is currently floating. Futures firms such as Apex, Topstep and MyFundedFutures generally have no daily loss limit on the evaluation, so the preset shows "none".

Consistency rules delay you; they rarely fail you

A consistency rule says your best single day may not be more than a set share of your total profit – 50% at FTMO and MyFundedFutures, 55% of the profit target at Topstep, 40% on several funded accounts. If your best day is 60% of your total profit, the rule does not fail you; it means you need more profit on other days before the best day "fits". The calculator shows that number so you know how far the pass really is.

FAQ

What is the difference between static and trailing drawdown?

Static fixes the floor at starting balance minus max loss and it never moves. Trailing moves the floor up as your balance (end-of-day) or your equity including open profit (intraday) makes new highs. Locked trailing stops at the starting balance or starting balance plus $100.

Does the daily loss limit include open trades?

At FTMO and FundedNext, yes – floating losses count against the balance at the daily reset. A trade that is deep in drawdown at the reset can breach the limit even if it recovers later.

Does a consistency rule fail my challenge?

Usually it delays your pass or payout until the other days have added enough profit. Some funded accounts hold a payout until the rule is met.

Are the presets guaranteed to match my firm?

No. They were checked in September 2026 against published rules, but firms change them. Your dashboard is the final word; use Custom if your plan differs, and tell us if a preset is wrong so it gets fixed.
